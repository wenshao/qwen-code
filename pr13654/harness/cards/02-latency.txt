## Why
== PublicationClient still awaits every operation in order (one 1 MiB segment at a time) and, after a 202,
== sleeps a fixed 250 ms before its first status GET. A 1 MiB segment verifies in ~30-60 ms, so each segment
== now costs >= 250 ms instead of ~40 ms; 512 MiB = 513 serialized segments.
