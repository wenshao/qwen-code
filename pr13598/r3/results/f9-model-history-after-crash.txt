== F9: what the model is sent after a crashed automation turn is consumed
Each line is one chat request: (role, content) after the system prompt. 'schedule:<slot>' = the scheduled-run text of that occurrence.
Two occurrences in one user message means the crashed run's unanswered user record was merged into the next turn's prompt.
