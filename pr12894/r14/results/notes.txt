Notes on the round 14 result files

s20-r14b-o.log / s20-r14b-l.log
  First version of s20-unstarted.ts. Its "journal ... contains" check looked in
  qwen_managed_session_journal_tx, but message bodies live in
  qwen_managed_session_resource (the journal only carries a contentRef), so the
  "contains ...=false" values there mean nothing. s20-r14b2-o.log and later runs
  read the resource table and reload on a new Harness process.

s20-r14real2-o.log
  REAL2=1: after the fake-model turn the Session is loaded on a Harness backed by
  real qwen3.8-max, through a recording forwarder. "provider requests" counts
  escaped lone surrogates (\udXXX not part of a pair), U+FFFD and raw emoji in the
  request body. The display of "head ..." in model-saw lines is a 60-unit slice made
  by the probe itself and can split a pair on its own; the lone-surrogate counts are
  computed on the whole string.

  Prompt: "Wait 3 seconds, then print the current date with the date command. Do it
  in a single shell command." Round 13's O2 runs on 42e2e8b1 took 11 calls each.
